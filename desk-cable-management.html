The trade-off

The VIVO tray is the better fix if you've got a genuine rat's nest under your desk — a power strip, a monitor cable, a charger, and more all tangled together. It takes up more physical space and needs a flat desk edge to clamp onto, but it hides everything completely. The adhesive clips are a lighter-touch fix: they won't hide a power strip, but they'll stop two or three cables from sliding around or falling behind the desk.

If your under-desk area has multiple devices and a power strip, the tray is worth the setup. If it's just a couple of charging cables you want kept in place, the clips are the simpler, cheaper fix.

What we're not recommending, and why

We avoided cable management products that require permanent drilling or adhesive strong enough to damage the desk surface on removal — a genuine risk for anyone renting or using shared office furniture. Both our picks are fully removable without residue or damage.
