The trade-off

The trtl wins decisively on actual neck support for long-haul flights — it's designed by a physiotherapist specifically to stop your head lolling forward, which memory foam alone doesn't solve. The Amazon Basics pillow can't match that structural support, but it's genuinely comfortable, machine washable, and costs a fraction of the price — perfectly fine for a two-hour train journey or car nap.

If you're flying long-haul and actually need to sleep upright, the trtl's support structure is worth the extra cost. For shorter trips, commutes, or occasional use, the Amazon Basics pillow does the job without the premium price tag.

What we're not recommending, and why

We avoided inflatable neck pillows despite their compact size. They're a reasonable space-saver, but reviewers consistently report they lose firmness within the first hour — exactly when you need support most on a long flight.
