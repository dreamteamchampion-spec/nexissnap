Best Packing Cubes for UK Travellers (2026)

Updated 17 August 2026

Packing cubes live or die on two things: whether the zips survive months of being crammed into an overhead locker, and whether the fabric holds up after repeated washes. Rather than list every option on Amazon, we compared independent hands-on reviews and Amazon's own customer feedback to land on two picks that cover the two things most UK travellers actually care about — solid all-round value, and maximum space-saving.

The trade-off

The Amazon Essentials set is the safer buy for most people: simple, reliable, and cheap enough that replacing a torn cube isn't a big deal. The Gonex compression set earns its price with genuinely useful extra packing space, but that comes from a second zip mechanism — one more moving part that can fail with heavy use over time.

If you travel a handful of times a year and want something that just works, the Amazon Essentials set is the sensible default. If you're consistently packing light — carry-on only, weekend trips, budget airline hand luggage limits — the Gonex compression set's extra space is worth the trade-off.

What we're not recommending, and why

We deliberately avoided sets whose main selling point was an oversized bundle count (10+ pieces) at a rock-bottom price. In our research, most travellers end up using 4–6 cubes regardless of how many come in the box, so a larger set rarely means better value — it usually just means thinner fabric to hit the price point.
