The trade-off

The PROWORKS bottle is the better pick if you actually want cold water to stay cold through a full working day — the vacuum insulation genuinely holds up. The Amazon Basics bottle trades some of that insulation performance for a bigger 750ml capacity and a lower price, which matters more if you're topping up less often and just want volume.

If cold retention through a full day matters to you, the PROWORKS bottle is worth the smaller capacity. If you'd rather have more water on hand and top up less often, the larger Amazon Basics bottle is the more practical everyday choice.
